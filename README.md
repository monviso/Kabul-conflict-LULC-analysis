This scripts reproduce the analysis for the paper : "Urbanization in the Age of Recurring Conflict: Evidence from Afghanistan"\
The datasets for step 1-3 are available at Zenodo repositories.
- Landsat Predictors for Step1: https://figshare.com/s/68da2258e6d83be52c78 (all metrics were extracted from Google Engine). 
- Kabul LULC area change and conflict predictors for Step2: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18978730
- Natural and instituitonal services locations for Step3: https://figshare.com/s/68da2258e6d83be52c78
